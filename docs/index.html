Slotfill

Turning a scanned receipt into a record means filling four slots (shop, address, date, total) with values that pass a strict schema: a real calendar date, a positive amount with two decimals. Here hand-written rules and a small trained extractor over OCR lines, with their positions on the page, are compared on 200 held-out receipts, against the ceiling of what the OCR text allows.
